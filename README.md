# The Midas band

Everything you touch turns to control...

Project Overview

The Midas Band is a wearable input device designed to replace the traditional mouse with mappable hand gestures and finger movements for anything from simple mouse controls to shortcuts for softwares such as blender etc.

The goal is to develop a compact, wireless wearable that allows users to control their devices in a more natural way, without needing a physical mouse or traditional input surface.
Combining custom PCB design, motion sensors, embedded programming and 3D-printed mechanical components, the Midas Band aims to bind the boundary of human-computer interaction.
Along with a custom app to map and control the band and add custom gestures, settings, profiles and check the battery of the band.

The Concept

A multi-use input controller, turned into a sleek and everyday wearable for higher productivity, minimalism and gesture controls for speed and expertise.
The Midas Band will be worn around the wrist, using a combination of sensors and gesture recognition to translate physical movements into digital commands.

Features

- Motion Tracking: IMU (accelerometer + gyroscope) for precise hand movement tracking.
- Finger Gestures: Due to the EMG electrodes built into the band, we are able to turn finger movements into digital inputs
- Gesture Recognition: Recognise specific hand and finger movements as computer commands.
- Wireless Connectivity: Bluetooth connectivity only, no clunky wires
- Cursor Control: Move the cursor through natural hand movements or on a flat surface with the optical sensor.
- Hand Control Control the cursor midair with built in IMG
- Gesture-Based Clicking: Configure your index finger movement and middle finger to left-click, right-click and even scrolling.
- Custom Shortcuts: Assign gestures to keyboard shortcuts and other computer functions.
- Custom Software: Configurable controls, sensitivity and settings profiles.

Engineering & Development

The project will involve designing and manufacturing the device from the ground up, rather than assembling existing off-the-shelf products.

| **Engineering Field** | **Development** |
|---|---|
| PCB Design | Custom-designed PCB integrating the microcontroller, sensors and other electronics |
| Electronics | IMUs, microcontrollers, batteries and wireless communication |
| Programming | Embedded C/C++, sensor fusion and gesture-recognition algorithms |
| CAD Design | Custom 3D-modelled enclosure, wristband and internal mounting systems (DEV MVPs will have no enclosure) |
| Manufacturing | PCB fabrication, soldering, 3D printing and assembly |
| Software | Computer interface, configuration tools and Bluetooth HID integration |

Building the Band

1. Design and manufacture a custom PCB.
2. Develop accurate motion tracking and gesture recognition.
3. Implement wireless computer connectivity.
4. Design and 3D print a compact, ergonomic enclosure.
5. Develop firmware and supporting configuration software.
6. Produce a fully functional prototype capable of replacing a traditional computer mouse.

Final Product

The ultimate objective is to create a polished, functional wearable that fundamentally changes how users interact with computers.
The Midas Band should be compact, responsive, comfortable and intuitive, demonstrating how custom electronics, mechanical engineering and software development can combine to create a new form of computer use. Also be comfortable and have a 3d printed clasp mechanism to open it up
